Left stick: forward/back and turn. Right stick: sideways and up/down. Tilt: pitch and roll. LB closes the claw, RB opens it. B changes ROV, X changes camera, Y switches pitch and roll between tilt and level, Start resets. Keyboard: arrows, W A S D, T F G H, "," and "." for the claw, B, X, Y, Enter. V shows forces, Q changes drawing resolution, I shows what the sim receives.
